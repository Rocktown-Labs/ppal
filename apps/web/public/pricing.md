# ParlayPal pricing

ParlayPal is a sportsbook-independent live bet companion. All plans include an account dashboard and secure slip processing. ParlayPal does not accept wagers or operate a sportsbook.

## Free

- Price: $0 forever
- Includes: 5 tracked tickets per month, 15-minute live updates, one historical slip upload, basic player hit rates, and in-app notifications.
- CTA: [Create a free account](https://myparlaypal.com/login)

## Pro

- Price: $12.99/month or $99.99/year ($8.33/month billed annually)
- Includes: 500 ticket uploads per month, five-minute live updates, player/team/market analytics, streak and ticket-size insights, email game alerts, and priority processing.
- CTA: [Start with ParlayPal](https://myparlaypal.com/login)

## Creator

- Price: $24.99/month or $199/year ($16.58/month billed annually)
- Includes: 1,000 ticket uploads per month, bulk historical archive imports, highest-priority processing, creator profile analytics, and all Pro features.
- Creator communities: Active Creator subscribers can create public or private communities with channels, paid or free membership, moderation, and realtime chat.
- CTA: [Start with ParlayPal](https://myparlaypal.com/login)

Prices and included limits were last reviewed September 2026. Check the live pricing section for the current offer before subscribing.
